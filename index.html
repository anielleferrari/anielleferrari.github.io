About Me

Hello! I’m Anielle Ferrari, a microbiologist and bioinformatician. My research focuses on bacterial genetics, cyclic nucleotide signaling, and genome analysis. I enjoy combining experimental and computational approaches to understand microbial processes.

Research

My main research projects include genome assembly of novel bacterial species, in silico analysis of protein structures, and bioinformatic pipelines for functional annotation. I am also exploring regulatory networks mediated by c-di-AMP and other signaling molecules in bacteria.
