Affiliations: University of Sao Paulo (USP) – Institute of Biomedical Sciences (ICB)

I am a PhD candidate in Microbiology at the Institute of Biomedical Sciences (ICB), University of Sao Paulo (USP), supervised by Prof. Dr. Cristiane Rodrigues Guzzo in the Laboratory of Structure and Evolution of Proteins (LSEP) group. My research focuses on Leptospira interrogans and bacterial signaling via c-di-GMP.

My scientific interests include spirochetes, particularly Leptospira, as well as structural biology, dinucleotides and bacterial signaling pathways.

Outside of research, I enjoy coffee, animals, dancing, music, the beach, and, of course, I am a proud member of Ravenclaw.
